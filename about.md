**Sole Society** is a small, hand-done shoe cleaning shop that started at the
kitchen table with a brush, a towel and a pile of sneakers that had seen
better days.

It's run by **Calvin**: founder, CEO, head of cleaning, and (for now) the
entire staff.

The goal is simple: get your kicks looking as close to new as possible,
and show you exactly what changed with honest before &amp; after photos.

This website is open source. Found a typo or want to help? The code lives on
[GitHub](https://github.com/cyberoblivion/sole-society-site).
